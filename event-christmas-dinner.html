Annual Christmas Dinner

Wigilia away from home — a Polish Christmas Eve feast for 100 students.

Date8 December 2025
VenueOgnisko, South Kensington
Attendance100 students

Our annual flagship Christmas Dinner brought together 100 Polish students at the Ognisko Restaurant in South Kensington. Guests sat down to enjoy a traditional three-course meal, followed by drinks and Christmas carol singing — a taste of a Polish family Christmas for students spending the festive season away from home.

The evening has become one of the Federation's most loved traditions: a chance to slow down at the end of term, share kolędy with friends old and new, and celebrate the community we've built across the UK.

A special thank you goes to Roksana Dąbkowska, a pianist from the Guildhall School of Music and Drama, for accompanying our Christmas carols at the piano throughout the evening.
