2025 / 2026 Season

Our events

A year in the life of the Federation — from a 300-person business forum at London Business School to carol singing at Ognisko. Here's what we've built this year.

2025/26
★ Flagship event 24–25 April 2026 · London Business School

Polish Business Forum 2026

Over 300 students, professionals, investors and policymakers gathered under the theme “Polish Golden Age: From Emerging to Leading” — two days of keynotes, panels, a career fair and the inaugural Forum Ball.

Read more →
10 February 2026 · Polish Institute & Sikorski Museum

How to Think About Politics in a Polarised World

An academic debate on polarisation and disinformation, opened by a keynote from Professor Nicholas O'Shaughnessy and followed by an Oxford-style debate and guided tours of the Institute's collections.

Read more →
8 December 2025 · Ognisko, South Kensington

Annual Christmas Dinner

Our flagship Christmas celebration — 100 Polish students sat down to a traditional three-course meal at the Ognisko Restaurant, followed by drinks and Christmas carol singing.

Read more →
9 November 2025 · Ognisko Polskie, London

Polish Youth Congress 2025

A one-day conference with the Embassy of the Republic of Poland — panels on finance, leadership and geopolitics, closed with a Chopin recital in the spirit of Poland's National Independence Day.

Read more →
October 2025 · Mamuśka!, Waterloo

Icebreaker

The opening social of the year — around 200 Polish students met for drinks and conversation at Mamuśka! in London Waterloo, where the new committee was introduced.
